# IGRIS Account Creation Demo Guide

## Overview
This guide walks through the complete account creation and approval process on the IGRIS trading platform.

---

## Step 1: Navigate to Sign Up Page

1. Open the IGRIS application at your production URL
2. Click the **"Get Started"** button on the homepage
3. Or directly navigate to `/signup`

![Home Page → Get Started Button]

---

## Step 2: Enter Your Details

### Step 2a: Basic Information
1. **Full Name**: Enter your full name (minimum 2 characters)
2. **Email**: Enter a valid email address
3. Click **"Send Verification Code"**

### Step 2b: Email Verification (Optional)
1. Check your email for a 6-digit verification code
2. Enter the code in the verification field
3. Click **"Verify Email"**
4. *Note: You can skip verification by clicking "Skip verification for now"*

### Step 2c: Set Password
1. Create a password (minimum 6 characters)
2. Click **"Create Account"**

---

## Step 3: Account Pending Approval

After successful registration, you'll see:
- ✅ **"Account Created!"** confirmation
- ℹ️ **"Please wait for admin approval"** message
- 📩 Information that approval usually takes less than 24 hours
- 🔗 **"Back to Sign In"** link

**Important**: You cannot log in until an admin approves your account.

---

## Step 4: Admin Approval Process

### For Administrators:
1. Log in with admin credentials (`admin@igris.com`)
2. Navigate to **"Manage Users"** in the sidebar
3. Find users with pending approval status
4. Click **"Approve"** button next to the user's name
5. The user will receive approval notification

---

## Step 5: First Login After Approval

1. Navigate to `/signin`
2. Enter your registered email
3. Enter your password
4. Click **"Sign In"**
5. 🎉 You're now logged into the IGRIS dashboard!

---

## Troubleshooting

### "Account is pending approval" error
- Your account hasn't been approved yet
- Contact the administrator for approval

### "Invalid email or password" error
- Double-check your email spelling
- Ensure password is correct (case-sensitive)
- Click "Forgot Password" if needed

### "Email already registered" error
- You already have an account
- Use the Sign In page instead
- Or request a password reset

---

## Security Features

- 🔐 Passwords are securely hashed
- ✅ Admin approval prevents unauthorized access
- 📧 Email verification available for extra security
- 🚫 Accounts can be disabled by admin at any time
